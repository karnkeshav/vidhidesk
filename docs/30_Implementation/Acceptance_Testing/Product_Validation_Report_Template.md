**Severity guide** (consistent with the Product Constitution's Legal Safety Principles — a defect that could cause an advocate to rely on something false ranks above one that merely looks unpolished):
- **Critical** — a fabricated fact, statute, or citation presented as confirmed; a limitation or forum result that is confidently wrong in a way that could cause a missed deadline or wrong filing if trusted without independent verification.
- **High** — a real gap that degrades trust or completeness (e.g., a category the tool has no correct answer for at all, a known-wrong-but-conservative computation) but is unlikely by itself to cause a missed deadline or a filed-in-the-wrong-forum outcome if the advocate applies ordinary professional review.
- **Medium** — a gap in completeness or polish that a careful advocate would likely catch on review, but that costs review time or could mislead a less careful reviewer.
- **Low** — cosmetic, wording, or minor UX friction with no correctness implication.
